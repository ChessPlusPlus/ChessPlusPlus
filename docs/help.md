# Help

<details>

<summary>What we cannot help you with</summary>

Please note that there are some things that we are unable to help you with, which are inclusive of but not limited to:

* Help! I accidently digested the website. What do I do?
* Help! The website started talking to me in my sleep. How do I cure my insomnia?
* Help! I wasted an hour troubleshooting because I forgot my caps-lock was on. Could you add a caps-lock warning?
* Help! My baby child almost chocked on my laptop after seeing your website. How can I make it harder to swallow?
* Help! Your website is working exactly as intended. Is this normal?
* Help! My friend found a bug. He poured coffee on the website and it stopped working. Could you please fix this?
* Help! I set a piece's image to an image of pigeon, but I have a phobia of pigeons. How can I continue to use the website without having a panic attack?
* Help! I think I found a secret easter egg in your website. Whenever I turn my laptop upside down, the word become unreadable! How does this work?
* Help! I am addicted to your website because it is too fun. How do I find food while using it 24/7?
* Etc.

</details>

Click on the feedback button on the bottom right and provide your name and email if you feel like it.
