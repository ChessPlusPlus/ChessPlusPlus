# Playing A Variant

After having made your custom variant, you can play and test it!

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (19).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Click on "Play variant" to select a variant to play, then you can start playing.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (18).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

There, you can click on pieces to move them around and test out their functionalities.
